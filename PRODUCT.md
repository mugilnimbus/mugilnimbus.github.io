# Mugil's portfolio

## Platform

Web. Browser-native HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules with inline SVG drawings. A Node.js script serves and packages the static site. No rendering library is used.

## Users

| Visitor | What they need |
| --- | --- |
| Recruiter with a minute or two | A first impression of who Mugil is, the skills on offer, and one clear idea per project without effort |
| Technical reviewer | The system step by step in a Detailed view, the written story, the source, and Mugil's background |

## Purpose

Present Mugil Sivasamy Kalamani, whose background is physics and artificial intelligence, through 15 projects told as a self-playing series.

## Principles

- One idea per project first. Detail only on request.
- Start from a problem the visitor may have felt, then show the answer.
- Draw the eye to one point at a time.
- The viewer does no work to follow the story, and can switch project in one click.
- Every project gets equal access.
- Skills are named briefly in the intro; the episodes are the evidence.
- Only facts from the reviewed project descriptions and Mugil's resume. No invented results, deployments or links.

## Look

Full black with white, silver and grey only. One drawn object on stage. Handwriting for questions, notes and closing lines. Frosted glass only on controls and overlays.

## Accessibility

Keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced-motion stills, live-region narration with full sentences, and a plain-text version of every story that also works without scripts.
